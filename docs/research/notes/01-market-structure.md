# Structure and Economics of the Card and Payments Acceptance Market (Global + Brazil, as of Sept 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Source quality notes are given inline. "Background knowledge" items are explicitly labeled in Inferences and were NOT verified with a source in this session.

## 1. Roles and precise definitions (who does what) and how major players map to them

### Takeaway
A card payment involves the cardholder, the issuer (cardholder's bank), the card scheme/network (bandeira, which sets rules and routes/clears), the acquirer (credenciador, which holds the merchant relationship and settles to it), and the merchant. A sub-acquirer / PayFac (subcredenciador) sits between acquirer and merchant, onboarding merchants under its own acquirer relationship; in Brazil it is a regulated arrangement participant and, since Resolução BCB 522/2025 (effective 11 May 2026), must participate in centralized settlement (Núclea SLC) as both receiver and payer regardless of volume. Registradoras (CERC, TAG, CIP/Núclea) register card receivables. A Jupiter-like PSP is legally a subcredenciador in Brazil.

### Cited Findings
- Issuer: "a financial institution that gives credit and debit cards and associated lines of credit to cardholders." Acquirer: "a bank or financial institution that processes customer credit or debit card payments on behalf of the business and routes them through the card networks to the issuer." — [Stripe: card payment processing and settlement](https://stripe.com/resources/more/credit-card-payment-authorization-and-transaction-settlement-process)
- In clearing, the card scheme (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) plays "the role of facilitator" of the exchange of payment details between issuer and acquirer; the network sorts batches by issuing bank and nets settlement positions across acquirers. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/credit-card-payment-authorization-and-transaction-settlement-process)
- Subcredenciador (Circular BCB 3.886/2018): "o participante do arranjo de pagamento que habilita usuário final recebedor para a aceitação de instrumento de pagamento" but does not participate in settlement as a creditor before the issuer. Subcredenciadores are arrangement participants, must have contracts with the arrangement founder (instituidor, i.e. the bandeira), and must apply KYC/AML controls. — [Migalhas: BC disciplina a figura do subcredenciador](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/277839/banco-central-disciplina-a-figura-do-subcredenciador)
- Old rule (Circular 3.886/2018, Res. BCB 150/2021): all subcredenciadores had to join centralized settlement as receivers; they could opt out of paying merchants through CIP while 12-month volume was below R$ 500 million, and had 180 days to join after crossing it. — [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/277839/banco-central-disciplina-a-figura-do-subcredenciador); [RTM: liquidação centralizada](https://rtm.net.br/liquidacao-centralizada/)
- NEW rule: Resolução BCB 522/2025 (published 10 Nov 2025, compliance by 11 May 2026) makes participation in the Núclea-administered SLC (Sistema de Liquidação Centralizada) mandatory for subcredenciadores both as receivers of flows and as payers to end-user merchants, independent of volume. Operational requirements cited: report anticipated settlements (antecipações) to Núclea by D+1; unique transaction identifiers linking payments to chamber records; ICP-Brasil digital signatures (JWS, spec SLC0912); API integration with Núclea SLC v1 with HTTPS webhooks for async returns; max 1,000 merchants per message; risk, fraud and AML frameworks; non-compliant subcredenciadores have capture of new transactions suspended by the instituidores. — [NDM Advogados: Liquidação centralizada de subadquirente / Res. BCB 522](https://ndmadvogados.com.br/artigo/liquidacao-centralizada-subadquirente/) (law-firm summary; primary text is the BCB resolution, not fetched)
- A March 2026 BCB rule on payment arrangements was also published (details not fetched). — [Barcellos Tucunduva: BCB publica nova regra sobre arranjos de pagamento](https://barcellostucunduva.com.br/2026/03/22/bcb-publica-nova-regra-sobre-arranjos-de-pagamento/)
- Registradoras: CERC, TAG and CIP/Núclea are the BCB-authorized registries of payment-arrangement receivables, created under Resolução CMN 4.734/2019 and Circular 3.952/2019 (operational since 2021); all acquirer transactions are registered centrally, enabling receivables portability and use as collateral with any financial institution, with merchant consent. — [wfintechs: registradoras](https://wfintechs.substack.com/p/145-ptbr); [Blog do Inter: Registradora de recebíveis](https://blog.inter.co/registradora-de-recebiveis); [Núclea: registradora de recebíveis de cartões](https://www.nuclea.com.br/registradora-de-recebiveis-de-cartoes/)
- Brazilian acquirer ownership: Rede closed its capital in 2012 and was absorbed by Itaú; Getnet left the stock exchange in 2022 by Santander's decision; Cielo (Bradesco/Banco do Brasil) delisting was "a permanent point of discussion" per Bradesco's CEO. — [InvestNews: Destronada nas maquininhas, Cielo...](https://investnews.com.br/negocios/destronada-nas-maquininhas-cielo-luta-para-se-manter-relevante-na-era-do-pix/)
- The competitive battleground from 2025 shifted from acquiring itself to controlling the merchant's whole financial flow (business account, credit line, embedded anticipation); for big banks acquiring "deixou de ser negócio por si". — [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/negocios/destronada-nas-maquininhas-cielo-luta-para-se-manter-relevante-na-era-do-pix/); [InfoMoney: Nova batalha das maquininhas](https://www.infomoney.com.br/business/nova-batalha-das-maquininhas-mostra-que-para-bancoes-adquirencia-deixou-de-ser-negocio-por-si/)

### Inferences
- Role mapping (background knowledge, not verified this session): Cielo (Bradesco+BB), Rede (Itaú), Getnet (Santander), Stone, PagBank/PagSeguro and Mercado Pago are credenciadores (acquirers) in Brazil; Pagar.me is Stone group's online/e-commerce PSP brand; Stripe and Adyen are global PSPs—Adyen acts as its own acquirer in many markets (acquirer + gateway + processor in one), whereas Stripe acts largely as a PayFac/aggregator on top of partner acquirers in many markets. Verify Brazil licensing status of Stripe/Adyen before citing.
- Gateway vs orchestrator (definitions, background knowledge): a gateway only transmits/tokenizes the transaction to an acquirer/processor and does not hold funds; an orchestrator routes across multiple acquirers/PSPs (retries, cost/approval routing) without being merchant of record. A PayFac/sub-acquirer, in contrast, onboards merchants under its own MID, receives funds and pays out merchants — which is why it is regulated (SLC, registradoras) in Brazil.
- For Jupiter design: a Brazilian PayFac must model (a) merchant sub-accounts with KYC, (b) a receivables schedule per merchant that is registrable at registradoras, (c) settlement files/messages to Núclea SLC with unique IDs, and (d) anticipation events reported by D+1.

## 2. Message flow of a card payment (authorization, capture, clearing, settlement, funding; void vs refund vs reversal)

### Takeaway
Authorization is a real-time message (seconds) that places a hold but moves no money; capture turns it into a clearing record; clearing happens in batches, typically overnight; settlement between issuer→network→acquirer happens within 1–3 business days internationally, and merchant funding typically 2–3 business days (Brazil differs—see section 3). Before clearing you cancel/void via authorization reversal (full or partial); after clearing you refund via a separate message.

### Cited Findings
- Authorization: the processor asks the issuer via the network for approval with card, amount and verification data; the issuer returns an authorization code; "usually takes place within a few seconds"; the issuer immediately reduces available balance/credit. Authorizations "usually last 5 to 10 days but can sometimes last for up to 30 days". — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/credit-card-payment-authorization-and-transaction-settlement-process)
- Clearing: the acquirer batches the day's transactions and submits to the network, which sorts by issuer; the issuer verifies against authorization records and calculates interchange. Settlement: issuer → network → acquirer, network nets positions. "Clearing is completed overnight, and settlement is completed within one to three business days after the transaction. Funding takes two to three business days." — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/credit-card-payment-authorization-and-transaction-settlement-process)
- Authorization validity windows (Stripe): card-not-present CIT — Visa 7 days, Mastercard 7, Amex 7, Discover 7; Visa MIT 5 days; card-present — Visa 5 days, Mastercard/Amex/Discover 2 days; Stripe's exact operational window is "4 days and 18 hours" for the 5-day cases to allow clearing time. Extended authorizations available for eligible online and Terminal payments; Japan JPY up to 30 days. Expired authorizations release funds and the PaymentIntent becomes `canceled`. — [Stripe docs: Place a hold on a payment method](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method)
- Partial capture: capture less than authorized via `amount_to_capture`; "a partial capture automatically releases the remaining amount"; generally only one capture per authorization (multicapture for some cards); overcapture possible for some online card payments. Void = cancel the PaymentIntent before capture. — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method)
- Increase (issuer-side view): authorization = "please hold $X", no money moved; incremental authorizations raise the hold (e.g., tips); "An authorization can be either partially or fully reversed before the payment clears"; a card payment can have multiple `card_settlement` entries; "When a payment is refunded, Visa sends a separate card_refund message"; authorizations expire automatically per Visa validity rules; $0 card validation exists. — [Increase: Card payment lifecycle](https://increase.com/documentation/card-payment-lifecycle)

### Inferences
- Precise vocabulary for Jupiter's state machine: `authorized → (partially_)captured → cleared/settled → paid_out`, with `voided` (auth reversal pre-clearing, no fee to cardholder, hold released) vs `refunded` (post-settlement credit, new message, interchange normally not returned) vs chargeback (issuer-initiated dispute). In Brazil, "cancelamento" in acquirer APIs often covers both void (same day) and refund (after D0), handled differently in the agenda de recebíveis (refund debits future receivables).
- Messaging standard between acquirer and network is ISO 8583 (background knowledge; not sourced here).

### Gaps
- Brazil-specific authorization validity rules for Elo/Hipercard and national-scheme clearing times not found.
- Chargeback timelines/reason codes not researched (likely covered by another researcher).

## 3. Money flow and settlement cycles (Brazil D+30 per installment, debit, prepaid; international T+1/T+2; agenda de recebíveis)

### Takeaway
Brazil's credit card acquiring settles to merchants on D+30 for à vista credit and, for parcelado lojista, each installment at D+30, D+60, …; parcelado emissor pays the merchant the full amount on the D+30 cycle while the issuer finances the cardholder. Debit settles D+1 (background knowledge). This long credit cycle creates the "agenda de recebíveis" (the merchant's scheduled future receivables), which is registered at registradoras and is the base for anticipation (antecipação) — a core revenue line of Brazilian acquirers. Internationally, settlement is T+1–T+3.

### Cited Findings
- Parcelado lojista: merchant "irá receber cada parcela mês a mês, a iniciar D+30 … e ao final, receberá o valor da compra menos o MDR acordado com a adquirente para aquela quantidade de parcelas"; parcelado emissor: interest paid by the cardholder to the issuer, merchant receives the full value at once. — [Stone docs: Modalidades e tipos de parcelamento](https://online.stone.com.br/docs/tipos-de-transa%C3%A7%C3%B5es) (search snippet; also [Finanzero](https://finanzero.com.br/blog/qual-a-diferenca-entre-parcelado-lojista-e-parcelado-emissor/))
- Interest-free installments (parcelado sem juros) represented 42.6% of card purchases in 2025; up to 6 installments is most common (64.2% of installment purchases). — [Panorama Abecs 2025](https://panoramaabecs.com.br/economia-pagamentos-cartoes-brasil-2025-dados-abecs/)
- BCB's planned credit interchange consultation "will not determine changes in the settlement deadlines" for credit card transactions (i.e., the D+30 structure stays). — [Finsiders: BC vai lançar consulta pública](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/congresso-abipag-2025/bc-vai-lancar-consulta-publica-para-limitar-tarifas-de-intercambio-de-cartoes/); [Contábeis](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/71552/bc-avalia-teto-para-taxa-de-cartao-de-credito-no-comercio)
- Agenda de recebíveis: registered at CERC, TAG and CIP/Núclea since Res. 4.734; receivables became a standardized, portable asset usable as collateral with any financial institution with merchant authorization. — [Blog do Inter](https://blog.inter.co/registradora-de-recebiveis); [wfintechs](https://wfintechs.substack.com/p/145-ptbr)
- Subcredenciadores must report anticipated settlements to Núclea by D+1 under Res. BCB 522/2025. — [NDM Advogados](https://ndmadvogados.com.br/artigo/liquidacao-centralizada-subadquirente/)
- International: settlement 1–3 business days, merchant funding 2–3 business days. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/credit-card-payment-authorization-and-transaction-settlement-process)

### Inferences
- Debit D+1 and prepaid settlement terms (typically D+1/D+2 in Brazil) are background knowledge; not confirmed with a source here.
- In Jupiter, each captured credit transaction of N installments should generate N receivable units (unidades de recebível) with due dates D+30·k, net of per-installment MDR; anticipation = discounting those units at a monthly rate; refunds/chargebacks net against future units.

- Primary BCB/arrangement text for settlement deadlines (D+30 credit, D+1/D+2 debit) not fetched.
- Whether any 2026 initiative shortened credit settlement in Brazil: none found; only the statement that the interchange consultation won't touch it.

## 4. Economics (MDR, interchange, scheme fees, acquirer margin, PSP pricing, anticipation)

### Takeaway
MDR = interchange (to issuer) + scheme fee (to bandeira) + acquirer/PSP margin. In Brazil, average credit MDR was 2.16% and credit interchange 1.68% in 1H2025 (≈0.5 pp left for scheme fees and acquirer margin); debit MDR 1.08% vs debit interchange at the 0.50% cap; prepaid interchange at its 0.70% cap. BCB caps debit/prepaid interchange since April 2023 and has signaled a consultation on capping credit interchange (no final rule found as of Sept 2026). Anticipation fees (~2–3.5% a.m. per low-quality sources) are a large profit pool.

### Cited Findings
- BCB statistics, first halves of 2023/2024/2025: average credit MDR 2.32% → 2.22% → 2.16%; average debit MDR 1.14% → 1.10% → 1.08%. Average credit interchange (TIC) 1.60% (2023) → 1.64% (2024) → 1.68% (1H2025). Debit and prepaid TIC stayed near the caps, 0.50% and 0.70%. — [Bahia Econômica, citing BCB data, Nov 2025](https://bahiaeconomica.com.br/wp/2025/11/10/tarifas-de-intercambio-medias-praticadas-no-mercado-de-cartoes-seguem-estaveis/); primary datasets: [BCB open data – MDR](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/estatisticas-meios-pagamentos/resource/6d7e8d02-7aa0-48d5-8d4d-ca3dc76d13c7), [BCB open data – TIC](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/estatisticas-meios-pagamentos/resource/8c5762e0-82b3-4288-96b0-8734f275265d)
- Resolução BCB 246/2022: from 1 April 2023, debit interchange capped at 0.5% per transaction (replacing prior weighted-average + per-transaction limit) and prepaid at 0.7% (initial proposal was 0.5% for both; 0.7% kept for prepaid to protect smaller fintech issuers). — [FGV Regulação em Números](https://regulacaoemnumeros-direitorio.fgv.br/post/banco-central-aprova-resolucao-que-disciplina-tarifa-de-intercambio-de-cartao-de-debito-e-pre); [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/2022/09/27/bc-limita-tarifa-de-intercambio-de-cartoes-e-o-que-isso-muda/); BCB's own evaluation: [Estudo Especial 106 – resultado regulatório do cap de débito](https://www.bcb.gov.br/conteudo/relatorioinflacao/EstudosEspeciais/EE106_Resultado_regulatorio_cap_cartao_de_debito.pdf)
- Credit interchange: BCB director Renato Gomes announced (2025) a public consultation on limiting credit card interchange, modeled on debit/prepaid; merchants support it, issuers/acquirers warn about effects on credit and installments. No finalized rule or consultation outcome found as of this research. — [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/congresso-abipag-2025/bc-vai-lancar-consulta-publica-para-limitar-tarifas-de-intercambio-de-cartoes/); [Investing.com BR](https://br.investing.com/news/world-news/banco-central-do-brasil-planeja-consulta-publica-sobre-limites-de-tarifas-de-cartao-de-credito-93CH-1567199); [Contábeis](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/71552/bc-avalia-teto-para-taxa-de-cartao-de-credito-no-comercio)
- BCB research: acquiring deconcentration and increased competition (lower Lerner index) explain the fall in debit MDR between 2018Q4 and 2020Q1; financial conglomerates held 46% of acquiring volume (REB 2023, older data). — [BCB Relatório de Economia Bancária 2023, box](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/publicacoes/boxe_relatorio_de_economia_bancaria/reb2023b6p.pdf)
- Interchange-plus: fee = interchange (issuer) + assessment/scheme fee (network) + processor markup; blended/flat-rate pricing merges all into one rate; fee categories are interchange, scheme fees, acquirer markup, gateway fees, FX fees. — [Stripe: Interchange plus pricing explained](https://stripe.com/resources/more/interchange-plus-pricing-explained); [Payrails](https://www.payrails.com/blog/payment-processing-fees-interchange-plus-vs-flat-rate); [PXP: blended pricing](https://www.pxp.io/payments-glossary/blended-pricing)
- Global benchmarks: US interchange ranges Visa 1.15%+$0.10 to 2.70%+$0.10; Mastercard 1.15%+10¢ to 2.60%+10¢; Amex 1.43%+$0.10 to 3.30%+$0.10; US businesses paid ≈US$187.2bn in card processing fees in 2024, interchange 70–90% of that; EU IFR caps consumer interchange at 0.20% debit / 0.30% credit (domestic and intra-EEA); US Durbin caps regulated debit at $0.21 + 0.05% for issuers >$10bn assets. Flow: network deducts interchange before passing funds to the acquirer. — [Stripe: Interchange fees 101](https://stripe.com/resources/more/interchange-fees-101-what-they-are-how-they-work-and-how-to-cut-costs)
- Pix cost to merchants averages 0.22% per transaction vs 2.2% for credit cards (EBANX/PCMI). — [Finsiders / EBANX](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/estudos-e-relatorios/em-2025-pix-ultrapassara-cartao-em-pagamentos-no-e-commerce-diz-ebanx/) (as summarized in search results)
- Anticipation: automatic anticipation (all future receivables paid D+1) "costs on average 1.99–2.49% a.m. in 2026 according to BCB data"; typical market range 2–3.5% a.m.; Stone charges per day anticipated per installment, customized by segment; PagBank offers sporadic and scheduled anticipation. — [Calculadora de Taxas](https://www.calculadoradetaxas.com.br/maquininha-com-antecipacao); [Stone Ajuda](https://ajuda.stone.com.br/antecipacao/antecipacao-e-seus-beneficios); [PagBank](https://pagbank.com.br/para-seu-negocio/vantagens-das-maquininhas/antecipacao-de-vendas). LOW-QUALITY: comparison sites; the "BCB data" attribution was not verified.
- The anticipation/fee-based acquiring model "is no longer the center of profitability gravity"; margins shifted to banks integrating credit, account and processing. — [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/negocios/destronada-nas-maquininhas-cielo-luta-para-se-manter-relevante-na-era-do-pix/)

### Inferences
- Implied Brazil credit "acquirer + scheme" slice ≈ 2.16% − 1.68% = 0.48 pp (1H2025); debit ≈ 1.08% − 0.50% = 0.58 pp. Credit's spread is shrinking (MDR falling while TIC rising), which pushes acquirers/PSPs to monetize anticipation, accounts and credit.
- Brazilian MDR is typically quoted per modality (débito, crédito à vista, parcelado 2–6x, 7–12x), plus separate anticipation; a Jupiter pricing engine should model per-modality MDR + per-installment fee + optional anticipation rate (monthly, pro-rata per day), and optionally IC++ for large merchants.

## 5. Market size and main players in Brazil; Pix vs cards vs boleto; 2024–2026 trends

### Takeaway
Cards moved R$ 4.5 trillion in Brazil in 2025 (+10.1%; credit R$ 3.1 tn), while Pix moved R$ 35.4 trillion (79.8 bn transactions) — Pix dominates transfers and has overtaken credit cards in e-commerce (42% vs 40% in 2025, Worldpay). Acquiring is concentrated among six players; by TPV in Jan 2026 Rede 18%, Cielo 17%, PagBank 17%, Stone 15%, Mercado Pago 9%, Getnet 5% (UBS BB). Bank-owned acquirers are regaining share.

## 6. Payment methods overview (credit, debit, prepaid, Pix, boleto, wallets, BNPL, Open Finance / Pix por aproximação / JSR)

### Takeaway
Beyond cards, Brazil's key rails are Pix (instant, ~0.22% merchant cost), with new modalities: Pix por aproximação via Jornada sem Redirecionamento (JSR, Open Finance-based, launched 28 Feb 2025), Pix Automático (recurring, launched 16 Jun 2025); BCB abandoned regulating "Pix Parcelado" in Dec 2025. Wallets are ~10% of e-commerce, projected 15% by 2030.

### Cited Findings
- Pix por aproximação: broad launch on 28 Feb 2025, NFC payments enabled by the Jornada Sem Redirecionamento (JSR); Google Wallet first to offer it (pilot, Nov 2024) for C6 Bank and PicPay customers. — [Instituto Propague](https://institutopropague.org/pagamentos/pix-por-aproximacao-tem-regras-divulgadas-e-deve-ser-lancado-em-fevereiro-de-2025/); [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/pagamentos/comeca-piloto-da-jornada-que-vai-permitir-o-pix-por-aproximacao/); [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/bc-regula-funcionamento-da-jornada-sem-redirecionamento-que-viabiliza-pix-por-aproximacao)
- Pix Automático launched 16 June 2025 for recurring charges (utilities, school fees, gyms, health plans, streaming, subscriptions). — [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202506/pix-automatico-chega-em-16-de-junho); [Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2024-07/bc-define-que-pix-automatico-sera-lancado-em-junho-de-2025)
- Pix Parcelado: concept = payer takes credit to split, receiver gets full value instantly; after repeated delays BCB decided (4 Dec 2025, Fórum Pix) to abandon specific rules for it. — [Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2025-12/apos-adiamentos-banco-central-desiste-de-regular-pix-parcelado)
- Wallets 10% of Brazil e-commerce (2025) → 15% (2030); debit 4% → 2%. — [Poder360 / Worldpay GPR 2026](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/pix-chega-a-42-dos-pagamentos-on-line-no-brasil/)
- Contactless is 73.6% of in-person card purchases (2025). — [Panorama Abecs](https://panoramaabecs.com.br/economia-pagamentos-cartoes-brasil-2025-dados-abecs/)
- Separate auth/capture support varies by method: cards, Affirm, Afterpay, Klarna, PayPal support it (e.g., Klarna 28 days, PayPal 10+10 days, Afterpay 13 days); ACH/iDEAL do not. — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method)

### Inferences
- For Jupiter: Pix is push (no auth/capture, no chargeback but MED refund mechanism), so the payment model needs a method-agnostic PaymentIntent with method-specific state machines (card: auth/capture; Pix: pending→paid via webhook; boleto: registered→paid D+1–D+3).
- "Pix Parcelado" as offered in market is issuer/fintech credit, not a BCB product — model it as a BNPL-style method, not a Pix variant.
